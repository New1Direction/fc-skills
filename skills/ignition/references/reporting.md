# Reporting and handoffs

Lead with what changed by the cutoff and which observations merit investigation. A shortlist is a research priority, not a buy recommendation.

Use a compact table with exact token identity, age/unknown age, prior/current buyer breadth and spending, concentration, filtering sensitivity, candidate state, and coverage. Keep numeric components and flags separate. Include contrary evidence and precise missing measurements. State the collection method and available-at cutoff in the receipt.

Handoffs include candidate_id, chain_id, token_id, quote_unit, cutoff, source hash/locator, claim type and unresolved questions. Another skill must resolve those fields into its own input schema; native JSON schemas are independent and are not interchangeable by renaming a file.

- Autopsy: check launch allocation, early inventory and evidence for wallet relationships.
- Meme Scout: examine observed authors, community assignments and competing token identities.
- Scout Network: evaluate whether wallet track records were knowable at signal time and remain followable.
- Second Wind: use longer baseline history when the asset is beyond the early window.
- Exit Doctor: check supported execution paths and position-size costs at a relevant state. Its native V2 adapter does not establish V3/V4/Solana execution.

For an outcome review, show the full evaluated universe, missing outcomes, losses, execution failures, following delay, entry/exit rules, basis and costs. Compare against a simple predeclared screen and retain every alert. Maximum future price is not an executable return. The native journal supplies recordkeeping and grouped outcomes, not automatic market replay or benchmark collection.
