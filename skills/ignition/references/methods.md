# Traction methods

Measure buying quote expenditure, sell proceeds, unique buying addresses, size concentration and changes across comparable windows. Gross buy spending is not net external capital: a participant can sell elsewhere, reuse funds, arbitrage or route through several pools. Even buy-minus-sell quote flow does not measure new money entering the ecosystem.

Keep the exact quote asset and decimals. Compare a token's windows in the same unit; avoid treating different stablecoin symbols as one asset. When translating a stock-paired token into dollars, separate the stock token's move, its on-chain basis and the memecoin's exchange rate. Missing conversion evidence remains unknown.

Address-level breadth is a starting measure. Supply relationship hypotheses with evidence, keep shared infrastructure funding distinct from claimed common control, and show sensitivity rather than declaring a count of independent people. Common funding can be a service; tight timing can be shared public information.

Analyze activity before and after supplied suspected-bot labels and rapid opposite-side patterns. The native round-trip flag is a timing heuristic, not proof that equal inventory closed or that wash trading occurred. Keep filtered observations in the reported denominators.

Measure acceleration over equal durations and comparable coverage. Zero activity in a complete prior interval differs from a missing prior interval; do not produce infinite percentage growth. Token age requires sourced creation/first-tradability evidence, not the oldest swap in a limited query.

Cutoff checks apply to events, labels, relationship hypotheses and source evidence. An old trade discovered later was unavailable to the earlier observer. A label assigned after a token pumped must not change a historical screen. Keep rule versions and source hashes with each result.

Retention requires genuine cohort inventory measurements, acquisition/disposal accounting and matured windows. The native analyzer leaves this uncomputed. A purchase is not a current holding, and a transfer is not an independently identified purchase.

The initial screen is a transparent heuristic. Thresholds and filtering choices need chronological evaluation before being described as useful predictors. A candidate can have rising demand and severe insider distribution or poor execution. Obtain those separate measurements without letting a favorable measure erase contrary evidence.

Do not multiply attention, wallet and price indicators as if independent; they may describe one promotion or the same buyers. Do not call a condition causal merely because it preceded a price move.
