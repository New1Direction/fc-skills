# Optional handoff

Attach the result JSON, input SHA-256, source kind, cutoff, exact identities, evidence references, comparable window definitions, coverage, and unresolved exclusions. Do not forward synthetic examples as live evidence.

- **Ignition:** Assess present early traction or continuation after the revival hypothesis is stated. Preserve new versus returning cohorts and do not erase prior dormant history.
- **Autopsy:** Investigate whether launch allocation, concentrated incumbents, funding links, or fee privileges explain apparent resurgence. Supply exact chain/token and suspected relationships with confidence and alternatives.
- **Exit Doctor:** Evaluate a user-specified exit size and supported route when liquidity quality affects the decision. Reserve/depth growth here is not an executable sale quote or simulation. Let the recipient skill verify venue support and quote freshness.

If a skill is unavailable, identify the remaining research question in the report. No installation, trade, transaction, or external message is implied by a handoff.
