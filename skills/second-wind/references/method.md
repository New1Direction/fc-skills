# Method and interpretation

The native analyzer compares the same exact chain/token/token-unit/quote-unit/pool scope in equal-duration, nonoverlapping windows. At least one active-history window and two dormant windows must precede the current window, in that order and with no gaps between consecutive windows. Extra/unavailable windows do not silently count as zero. Retain a longer history where possible and disclose selection; the helper cannot prove that a researcher selected representative windows.

Each dormant window must have at most 10 trades/day and no more than 10% of the active-history mean trade rate and quote-volume rate. The active baseline must have positive trades and volume. Current resurgence requires at least 20 trades/day and both trade and volume rates at least 3 times the dormant mean. These configurable thresholds are **uncalibrated engineering defaults**, not empirical forecasting rules. Daily rates use actual elapsed seconds; ratios compare equal-duration windows. A zero dormant baseline returns an explicit positive-from-zero state and no numeric ratio. It must still pass the absolute trade-rate floor and positive-volume requirement.

Dormancy is established entirely from windows ending before current starts. Incomplete coverage, missing metrics, future publication times, uncertain comparability, or sequence gaps make dormancy indeterminate. A complete window declares exhaustive observation for its exact scope; an empty provider response by itself never establishes completeness.

Current buyer observations require a supported token-wide first purchase timestamp. With complete token purchase history, first purchase within the current window means new; an earlier purchase means returning. Without that history, cohort is unknown. A transfer, router address, airdrop, new wallet, or new address funded by a related incumbent does not alone identify a genuine new buyer. Normalize end-user buyers, filter routers and non-purchase transfers upstream, and retain the extraction rule. Buyer rows aggregate that wallet's positive gross buy consideration in the declared quote unit; selling does not subtract from capital entered. Recycled funds and wash trades can still inflate gross flows.

The participation check needs complete buyer coverage, complete first-purchase history, at least 3 new buyers, and complete declared relationship review. For new buyers, known suspected dependency groups count once; explicitly reviewed unlinked wallets count separately. Require at least 3 resulting groups and largest group's share of new-buyer quote capital no greater than 50%, and new buyers contributing at least 25% of all observed gross buy quote capital. This prevents a handful of tiny first purchases from confirming a large flow dominated by returning buyers. The count is a scenario under supplied grouping, not verified independent people. Report its limitations; regroup plausible related wallets and see whether the assessment changes.

Incumbents are wallets selected by a retained snapshot dated at or before current start. Show incumbent buys, sells, and net sells in quote units. Positive net sells flags distribution pressure; no incumbent evidence means unknown. Amounts are scoped gross swaps, not realized profit, portfolio exits, or beneficial ownership.

Liquidity improvement requires both quote reserves and fixed-slippage quote depth to rise within the current window, using the same quote asset, route scope, and slippage target. Token-unit liquidity growth is reported separately and never establishes this conclusion. Greater quote-valued token price, USD repricing of a quote asset, or added pool units without executable depth do not prove better exits. The native helper does not simulate execution or account for transfer taxes, hooks, blacklist rules, MEV, or price paths.

Classifications distinguish insufficient evidence, dormancy without resurgence, activity resurgence without confirmed new breadth, and resurgence with supported new participation (with an additional distribution-pressure label when applicable). They describe supplied observations only. Liquidity and distribution unknowns remain prominent even when participation is supported. No probability or trade recommendation is generated.
