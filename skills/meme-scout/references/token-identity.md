# Exact identity before market or chain handoff

Associate a token only through a specific chain namespace/reference and address. This helper accepts `eip155:<decimal-chain-id>` with a 20-byte hex address or `solana:<CAIP-2-reference>` with a base58 address decoding to 32 bytes. EVM addresses are lowercased for comparisons; Solana addresses preserve case. The helper checks syntax, not network existence, deployment, mint type, or checksum correctness. Unsupported chains require another verified adapter and must not be coerced into these formats.

Look for a project-controlled primary page, signed announcement, verifiable official account statement, or chain-explorer/source record explicitly connecting the narrative to the exact chain/address. Inspect primary content; a search title alone is insufficient. Record evidence URL, availability time, provenance, role, and a concise claim. A “verified” input is an analyst declaration; the helper only preserves it as `resolved_supplied` when eligible observed primary evidence exists. It never independently verifies the claim.

Multiple addresses with the same name remain separate candidates. Do not choose by market cap, launch order, logo, ticker, or posting frequency. Different chain deployments are distinct identities unless primary evidence establishes the relationship. `ambiguous` stays unresolved even when it has primary evidence; compare the conflicting claims in prose. Identity evidence unavailable by cutoff is excluded, and a later identity cannot resolve an earlier report.

Every observation's optional `token_ids` must reference tokens declared in the input. Those associations are supplied claims, not wallet ownership or endorsed token links. A matching X handle, ENS name, wallet label, or deployer name does not establish control; do not join social identities to wallets without separate verifiable ownership evidence.

Handoff schema `meme-scout.handoff.v1` contains the report schema, byte hash, provenance, window/cutoff, narrative identifier, exact token records, and retained post IDs. `token_id` is the unambiguous join key `<chain>/<address>`; send the separate `chain` and `address` fields too. Another skill can read this envelope without the Meme Scout package being installed. The receiving skill must apply its own chain, block/time, liquidity, venue, and evidence constraints; this envelope does not certify a token or recommend a trade.
