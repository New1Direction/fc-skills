---
title: "Meme Scout"
description: "See how a meme or story is spreading."
---

Use Meme Scout to find original participation, repeated promotion, and signs that a narrative is reaching new communities.

## What to provide

Saved posts with their text, links, timestamps, and source context. Include token-address evidence if you want to connect a meme to a coin.

## Ask your agent

```text
Use Meme Scout to review these posts.
Show where the narrative appears to spread beyond repeated promotion.
Include source links and gaps in the sample.
```

## What you get

A summary of the narrative, its observed spread, and any supported connection to a specific token.

You supply the posts or connect a data provider. This package does not include an X or Grok client.

<details>
<summary>Try a local example</summary>

**Runtime:** Python 3.12+.

Run from the repository root with a new output path. This example uses simulated data and makes no live network requests.

```sh
python3 skills/meme-scout/scripts/analyze.py skills/meme-scout/examples/synthetic-observations.json --out ../fc-meme-scout-report-new.json
```

Open the output to explore the report before adding your own data.

<summary>Technical details and source</summary>

- No native X collector, authenticated Grok client, credentials, or background social monitor is bundled.
- The helper does not infer sentiment, semantic near-copies, or independent people behind accounts.
- Sample counts are not an all-X census, bullishness score, or profit forecast.
- First observed in a sample is not first ever.
