---

Use Undertow to separate the meme’s performance from changes in its paired stock token. Add wallet-flow evidence to investigate observed rotation.

## What to provide

The exact token and pool, observation windows, comparable prices, and stock-token adjustment data. Flow analysis also needs attributed swap records.

## Ask your agent

```text
Use Undertow to explain this stock-paired memecoin’s move.
Separate meme performance from the stock-token move.
Only describe capital rotation where wallet-flow evidence supports it.
```

## What you get

A breakdown of price changes, supported flow observations, and shared liquidity relationships.

Price movement alone cannot establish where money moved. A pool mark is not a size-specific exit quote.

<summary>Technical details and source</summary>

- A V4 Swap sender or tx.from alone does not establish the beneficial trader.
- PoolManager custody is not one pool’s reserves; pool marks are not size-specific execution quotes.
- Current REST references require block-specific reconciliation before historical attribution.
- Relative performance is not causal alpha, shared quotes are not measured covariance, and V2/V3 adapters do not establish V4 support.
