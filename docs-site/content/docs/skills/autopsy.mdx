Use Autopsy to understand who received tokens early, where those tokens moved, and what the transaction history shows.

## What to provide

The chain, token address, launch period, and any saved transaction data. Your agent can identify which additional records it needs.

## Ask your agent

```text
Use Autopsy to investigate this token launch.
Show where the supply went, with transaction references.
Separate confirmed findings from possible explanations.
```

## What you get

A launch timeline, supply movements, wallet connections, and a list of missing evidence.

The built-in collector reads standard EVM token transfers. Shared funding alone does not prove that wallets have the same owner.

<details>
<summary>Technical details and source</summary>

- The native collector supports standard EVM ERC-20 transfers; it does not natively decode every swap, pool, trace, token tax, or Solana instruction.
- Transfers into a router or pool do not by themselves establish buys or sales.
- Shared funding and timing do not prove wallet ownership; incomplete history cannot establish a first-ever event.
- Structural report checks and hashes do not authenticate provider truth or certify a token.
