---
title: "Scout Network"
description: "Check whether a discovery wallet is worth following."
---

Use Scout Network to review a wallet’s past picks and whether another trader could realistically have followed them.

## What to provide

The full set of picks, including failures; when each became visible; and realistic follower delays, trade sizes, costs, and exits.

## Ask your agent

```text
Use Scout Network to review this wallet’s complete pick history.
Include failed picks and realistic follower delays and costs.
Show which results were actually followable.
```

## What you get

A review of discovery quality, follower results, and gaps that could distort the comparison.

There is no built-in live wallet collector. A list containing only successful picks cannot establish a reliable track record.

<details>
<summary>Technical details and source</summary>

- There is no native live wallet collector.
- The helper validates supplied normalized data; it cannot authenticate complete upstream history.
- Famous winners chosen after the fact cannot become a validated scout cohort.
- Missing exits/costs stay unknown, and wallet relationships do not prove common ownership.
