title: "PRESSURE"
description: "Follow changes in stock-token supply."
---

Use PRESSURE to investigate minting, burning, token destinations, and changes in measured liquidity.

## What to provide

The exact stock-token address, block window, supply and adjustment rules, and any address labels. Add comparable liquidity observations when available.

## Ask your agent

```text
Use PRESSURE to review supply changes for this stock token.
Show minting, burning, and observed destinations.
Compare available liquidity evidence and flag unknowns.
```

## What you get

A supply reconciliation, destination observations, and any supported liquidity comparisons.

Balances held by a custodian do not establish freely sellable supply. New issuance alone is not a sell signal.

node skills/pressure/scripts/pressure.mjs demo --out ../fc-pressure-demo-new

Open the output to explore the report before adding your own data.

<details>
<summary>Technical details and source</summary>

- Collection is bounded to up to 64 intervening blocks, 256 receipts, and 20 tracked addresses.
- Aggregate custody is not sellable float or complete V4 pool inventory; fungible units cannot be tracked after mixing.
- Liquidity evidence is supplied; PRESSURE does not build route calldata or quote unsupported pools.
- A mint is not an automatic sell signal, and an ordinary wallet cannot assume issuer mint/redemption privileges.
