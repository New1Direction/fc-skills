Use Agent Arena to understand performance while accounting for deposits, withdrawals, failed attempts, and open positions.

## What to provide

Portfolio snapshots, external cash movements, all trade attempts, costs, asset values, and liabilities. Include failed and pending attempts.

## Ask your agent

```text
Use Agent Arena to audit this agent’s performance.
Account for deposits, withdrawals, open positions, and failed attempts.
Show the evidence and any gaps that affect the result.
```

## What you get

A performance report and research journal showing funding effects, costs, open inventory, and unknowns.

You provide the history; there is no built-in live wallet collector. A report hash does not prove that an agent made the trades.

<details>
<summary>Technical details and source</summary>

- No native complete live wallet collector or realized cost-basis ledger is bundled.
- Trading fees and failed gas must already be reflected in complete NAV; their informational breakdown is not subtracted twice.
- Missing marks/liabilities or funding brackets prevent complete NAV or exact TWR.
- Journal hashes do not prove complete history, authentic timestamps, agent authorship, or future skill; no public leaderboard is deployed.
