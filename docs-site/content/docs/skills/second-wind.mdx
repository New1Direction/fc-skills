---

Use Second Wind to check whether a dormant coin is attracting new buyers and usable liquidity.

## What to provide

Activity from the token’s earlier active period, quiet period, and current period. Include buyer history, coverage, and comparable liquidity data.

## Ask your agent

```text
Use Second Wind to compare this token’s earlier and current activity.
Check new buyers, spending, liquidity, and selling by existing holders.
Show what supports or weakens a revival.
```

## What you get

A comparison of participation, buyer history, liquidity, and selling pressure across the token’s own past windows.

Incomplete history cannot establish that a buyer is new. The result is a research assessment, not a return forecast.

<details>
<summary>Technical details and source</summary>

- The bundled analyzer does not search live markets or provide an all-chain collector.
- Missing historical coverage cannot establish that a buyer is new.
- Migrations, rebases, or changed quote/token units need independent reconciliation; native comparisons reject these mismatches.
- A classification is not a revival probability or profit promise.
