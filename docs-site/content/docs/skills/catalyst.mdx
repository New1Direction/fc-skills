title: "CATALYST"

---

Use CATALYST to collect disclosures for selected companies and connect them to exact Robinhood Chain token and pool identities.

## What to provide

The companies to follow, exact security-to-token mappings, and any saved pool observations. Live SEC collection needs a configured User-Agent contact.

## Ask your agent

```text
Use CATALYST to review new filings for these companies.
Connect each filing to the exact stock token.
Separate the event date, filing date, and observed pool response.
```

## What you get

Filing facts, supported ownership details, publication timing, and any measured pool response.

Collection is limited to configured issuers. A price move after a filing does not prove the filing caused it.

<details>
<summary>Technical details and source</summary>

- Native discovery covers configured issuers and bounded filings, not every EDGAR event or global catalyst.
- A newly detected Form 4 can report an older transaction; reported shares times price is not proven cash paid.
- A pool response is not evidence that the filing caused the move or created tradable excess return.
- No sentiment LLM connector, automatic onchain publisher, production background deployment, or trade executor is bundled.
