Use WATCHTOWER to monitor Robinhood Chain, retain transactions and receipts, and send relevant activity to research workers.

## What to provide

An RPC provider, starting block, durable storage, and a running host. Add pool rules if you want specific research jobs.

## Ask your agent

```text
Use WATCHTOWER to monitor from this starting block.
Report missing blocks or receipts, source freshness, and worker progress.
Show any reports affected by a chain reorganization.
```

## What you get

Transaction and receipt records, coverage reports, recovery progress, and durable research jobs.

Coverage means transactions included in blocks reported by your provider. Private pending transactions and internal calls are outside native coverage.

<details>
<summary>Technical details and source</summary>

- Every transaction means every RPC-reported included transaction within the declared retained interval, not rejected submissions, all history since genesis, or private pending activity.
- Internal calls, internal native transfers, and revert reasons remain untraced without another verified adapter.
- Workers do not automatically execute other skills or submit Ape trades.
- No deployed host, sustained mainnet capacity, or fastest-provider result follows from installation or synthetic benchmarks.
