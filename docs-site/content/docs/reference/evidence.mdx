title: Evidence and coverage
description: Read a report without turning a local observation into a claim the data cannot support.
---

## Label what happened

| Evidence label | Meaning | Appropriate use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Synthetic | Fabricated identities or observations for teaching and validation | Check interfaces and expected behavior |
| Retained observation | Stored data with declared provenance, time, and coverage | Reproduce an analysis within its observed scope |
| Published illustrative | A documented external example with potentially incomplete history | Check supplied arithmetic, not authenticated replay |
| Modeled | A calculation under explicit assumptions | Compare scenarios and sensitivity |
| Simulated | A call or state transition measured in a declared environment | Assess that wallet call at that state and context |
| Executed | A real submitted transaction with retained execution evidence | Reconcile what actually happened and what it cost |

A retained RPC transcript checks reproducibility and consistency. It does not independently authenticate the provider. A file hash identifies content. It does not prove completeness or authorship.

## Carry identity and time together

For onchain research, preserve chain ID, exact contract and pool identity, block number and hash, observation time, and data source. For wallet-call evidence, also preserve wallet, calldata, trade size, route, and relevant approvals.

For disclosures, distinguish filing time, first observation, download completion, detail readiness, and the time an asset mapping became known. Do not use later mappings to manufacture a historical signal.

## Treat unknown values as unknown

- Missing costs are not zero costs.
- Custody balances do not establish sellable float or economic backing.
- Pool turnover is not independently attributed wallet participation.
- A quote or displayed mark is not a completed sale.
- A positive fork result is not realized profit or evidence that the route is currently available.
- A configured source is not evidence of complete or continuous collection.

## Preserve the comparison set

Keep complete candidate universes, failed attempts, missing outcomes, observation windows, external flows, and open inventory. Freeze point-in-time selection rules before evaluating outcomes. Compare like-for-like assets, state, timing, and costs.
