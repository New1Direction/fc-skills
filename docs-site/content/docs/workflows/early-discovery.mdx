title: Early memecoin discovery
description: Find research candidates through narratives, participation, wallet history, and usable liquidity.
---

## 1. Define the observed universe

Declare the tokens, venues, wallets, social sources, and time window you can actually observe. Retain unsuccessful candidates and missing outcomes so later evaluation does not only contain winners.

For Robinhood Chain intake, [WATCHTOWER](/docs/skills/watchtower/) can retain included transactions while [PULSE](/docs/skills/pulse/) compares data sources and maintains provisional pool observations. Those records still need decoding and attribution into the discovery skills' input schemas.

## 2. Find independent signs of adoption

- **[Meme Scout](/docs/skills/meme-scout/)** traces retained social posts and separates original adoption from amplification. Supply verifiable source posts; native live X or Grok collection is not included.
- **[Scout Network](/docs/skills/scout-network/)** evaluates discovery wallets using their complete historical picks and realistic follower delays and costs.
- **[Ignition](/docs/skills/ignition/)** compares normalized swap windows for participation, spend, and concentration. Use it to test whether attention is accompanied by broader buying.
- **[Second Wind](/docs/skills/second-wind/)** compares a dormant token with its own earlier windows to investigate renewed participation and incumbent selling.

These are complementary observations. Agreement is a reason to investigate further; it is not a calibrated probability of profit.

## 3. Investigate the launch

Use [Autopsy](/docs/skills/autopsy/) to reconstruct supply recipients, early transfers, funding evidence, liquidity changes, and exits. Its native EVM collector handles standard ERC-20 transfers. Supply independently retained swap, liquidity, and attribution evidence where required.

Keep wallet relationships as hypotheses unless supported. A shared funding source may have competing explanations.

## 4. Check the route and position size

Choose the adapter that matches the actual venue:

| Venue | Research path |
| --- | --- |
| Supported canonical 30-bps V2 route | [Exit Doctor](/docs/skills/exit-doctor/) |
| Robinhood V4 route | [HOOK LAB](/docs/skills/hook-lab/) deployment evidence, then [CIRCUIT](/docs/skills/circuit/) wallet-call construction and fork measurement |
| Unsupported venue or token behavior | Retain the gap and obtain a verified adapter before interpreting execution |

Freeze the candidate, observation time, decision rule, and follow-up window. Keep losses, unavailable observations, failed attempts, and costs in the outcome journal.

## Example agent request

```text
Review this declared candidate universe using Meme Scout, Scout Network,
and Ignition. Investigate the shortlisted launches with Autopsy.
For each candidate, show evidence provenance, identity confidence,
participation concentration, missing inputs, and the supported exit
research path. Preserve every candidate for later outcome evaluation.
```
